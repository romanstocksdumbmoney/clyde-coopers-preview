January 1, 1938

Fifty dollars & a pit

Clyde Cooper started Clyde Cooper’s Barbecue with fifty dollars in his pocket. The original building dated to 1884. After roughly fifty years he sold to a close friend — and kept coming back two or three times a week for jokes and barbecue until he died February 12, 1998, nearly 100.

Today Debbie & Randy Holt still cook shoulders the Eastern NC way — vinegar sauce, hush puppies, and a sell-out kitchen that aims to please, not leave you broken hearted.
